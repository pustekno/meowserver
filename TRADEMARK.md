# Trademark policy

The source code of MeowServer is free software under the GPL (see `LICENSE`
and `NOTICE`). The **name "MeowServer" and its logo** are not covered by that
license. This policy explains how they may be used.

## You may

- Say that your software works with, or is based on, MeowServer.
- Distribute **unmodified** official builds under the MeowServer name.
- Use the name and logo in articles, tutorials, talks, and reviews.

## You may not, without written permission

- Distribute a **modified** version under the name "MeowServer" or with its
  logo. Rename your fork and replace the logo.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests your product, service, or fork
  is official or endorsed by the MeowServer project, Pustekno, or Puskomedia.
- Bundle advertising, telemetry, or paid features into something that carries
  the MeowServer name.

Renaming a fork does not remove the attribution required by `NOTICE`: the
credit to Pustekno and Puskomedia must stay.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/pustekno/meowserver/issues.
